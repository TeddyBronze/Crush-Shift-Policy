Crush Shift

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

01 Overview

Crush Shift is a block-puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app and its third‑party services handle when you play it. There's no account, no sign‑in, and no personal profile — most of what follows concerns the advertising that keeps the game free.

02 Information we don't collect

We don't ask you to create an account or sign in, and we don't collect your name, email address, or any other information that identifies you.

Your progress — high score, stats, achievements, and similar game state — is stored only in your device's local app storage. It never leaves your device and we never see it.

03 Advertising

The game offers an optional rewarded video ad (a "Second Chance" to continue a run) through Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch it — nothing plays automatically. To serve and measure that ad, AdMob (and Google's ad partners) may collect information such as your advertising ID, IP address, general device information, and approximate location.

This collection is carried out by Google, not by us — we don't receive or store any of it ourselves. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for details.

04 Your choices

  • Reset or limit your device's advertising ID under your device's ad settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized advertising the same way — the game will still offer the rewarded ad, just not one based on your interests.
  • Skip the rewarded ad entirely by choosing not to watch it — it's never required to keep playing.

05 Children's privacy

Crush Shift is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. Ad requests are configured for a general audience rather than child‑directed treatment.

06 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
